Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Kitchen Mine provide in Pune?

Kitchen Mine provides premium modular kitchen design, modular furniture, wardrobes, TV units, storage solutions and Vedie-Vastu interior design services for residential and commercial spaces across Pune.

Why choose Kitchen Mine for a modular kitchen?

Kitchen Mine combines thoughtful design, practical storage, premium materials, quality craftsmanship and professional installation to create modular kitchens that are elegant, functional and tailored to your space.

Does Kitchen Mine offer a consultation?

Yes. Kitchen Mine offers a personalised consultation to understand your space, design preferences, requirements and budget before preparing a suitable kitchen or interior design proposal.

Does Kitchen Mine handle design and manufacturing?

Yes. Kitchen Mine handles modular kitchen and custom furniture design and manufacturing, helping maintain consistency in design, materials, finishing and craftsmanship.

Which areas of Pune does Kitchen Mine serve?

Kitchen Mine serves residential and commercial clients across Pune, including Baner, Balewadi, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Aundh, Kothrud, Viman Nagar, Koregaon Park, Hadapsar, Magarpatta and Pimpri-Chinchwad.
